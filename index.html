London · 2026

From Toronto to Montreal, New York, and London, my experiences have left me with a lasting interest in geopolitics, media, and the ways ideas and narratives travel across borders.

I am interested in questions that lie at the intersection of history and public life: how ideas gain legitimacy, whose voices carry authority, and how narratives shape political understanding.

This site is a portfolio of my research and professional work, and a home for the essays I write on my own time.
